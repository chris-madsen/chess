# Ubiquitous Language

This glossary is mandatory vocabulary for Chess Trainer. It prevents common mistakes such as calling a mixed-source teaching line a principal variation, treating Maia as best defense, or attributing a Stockfish continuation to LeelaAlien.

## Player

The human using Chess Trainer. The Player is the owner of strategic intent and final decision-making. The system may analyze, explain, challenge, and calculate, but ordinary analysis must not play the Player's move on an external platform.

## Opponent

The side the Player is preparing to face. In default HumanPath mode, the Opponent's moves are generated by Maia because the goal is practical play against likely human decisions.

## SideToMove

The side whose turn it is in a `PositionSnapshot`. Many policies depend on whether the Player is to move or the Opponent is to move. The system must not infer this from prose; it must be read from validated position data.

## PositionSnapshot

An immutable representation of one legal chess position. It includes:

- FEN or an equivalent canonical board state;
- side to move;
- castling rights;
- en-passant target if legal;
- halfmove/fullmove counters when available;
- optional PGN prefix or source reference;
- a stable hash of the canonical position.

A `PositionSnapshot` is the only valid starting point for scenario generation.

## LegalMove

A move that belongs to the legal move set of a specific `PositionSnapshot`. A string such as `Nf3` or `g1f3` is not a `LegalMove` until parsed and checked against the board state.

## AnalysisSession

A bounded conversation and computation around one `PositionSnapshot`. It contains Player reasoning, candidate seeds, requested horizon, generated lines, errors, and coaching review. It is not a game controller and does not submit moves.

## PlayerAssessment

The Player's own assessment of the current position. It can include:

- material balance;
- king safety;
- piece activity;
- pawn structure;
- weak squares and outposts;
- open files and diagonals;
- threats for both sides;
- initiative and tempo;
- endgame prospects;
- subjective evaluation and confidence.

The assessment may be incomplete or wrong; preserving it is useful for coaching.

## PlayerPlan

A strategic intention, not a forced line. Examples: attack the castled king, open the f-file, exchange into a favorable pawn ending, prevent a central break, improve the worst piece, or provoke a weakening move.

## CandidateSeed

One legal first move used to start a scenario. It is a single ply, not a variation. Allowed seed sources include:

- `PLAYER_IDEA`: the Player proposed the move;
- `LEELA_ALIEN_PROBE`: LeelaAlien actually played the move in a bounded probe from the same position;
- `BORIS_TRAPSKY_PROBE`: Boris-Trapsky actually played the move in a bounded probe from the same position;
- `IMPORTED`: the move came from a named external game, study, book, or puzzle record;
- `PUZZLE_TARGET`: the move is a known puzzle target in puzzle mode.

Every seed must carry `MoveProvenance`.

## LeelaAlien

A public Lichess bot commonly associated with unexpected, low-human-expectation move choices. Chess Trainer treats it as a source of practical candidate ideas only. The system must not claim LeelaAlien produced a line unless every move in that claimed line was actually observed from LeelaAlien in one game record.

## Boris-Trapsky

A public Lichess bot commonly associated with tricky and practical choices. Chess Trainer treats it as an optional source of one observed candidate idea, not as a full-line analysis provider.

## ExternalProbe

An opt-in, bounded interaction with an external bot or platform to observe an actual move. A valid probe record includes:

- provider identity;
- source position hash;
- game or request identifier when available;
- observed move;
- timestamp when available;
- quota/rate-limit metadata;
- outcome status.

External probes must not recursively branch game trees.

## Maia

A human-like model trained to predict moves that players at a target strength are likely to make. In HumanPath mode, Maia supplies Opponent moves. Maia output must be labelled as modeled human-likelihood, not objective best play.

## MaiaResponse

A legal Opponent move selected by the Maia adapter for a specific position and configuration. It must include target rating/model identity and selection settings.

## StockfishContinuation

A legal move generated by Stockfish 19 NNUE, usually for the Player's side after a Maia response. It represents strong calculation under a configured depth, node, time, skill, MultiPV, or other setting. It is advice data, not a command to move.

## AdversarialCheck

A separate, explicitly labelled mode where Stockfish searches for strong defensive resources or tactical refutations. It answers "can this practical idea be punished?" It must not replace Maia plies in a HumanPath line.

## ScenarioLine

A legal, ordered list of moves starting from a `PositionSnapshot`, used for teaching. The default `HumanPath ScenarioLine` alternates:

```text
CandidateSeed, Maia, Stockfish, Maia, Stockfish, ...
```

A `ScenarioLine` may be incomplete if a provider fails, a move is illegal, provenance is missing, or a terminal board state is reached.

## PrincipalVariation

A line produced by one engine in an engine-analysis mode under declared settings. Do not use this term for default HumanPath output.

## MoveProvenance

Immutable evidence attached to one move. Required fields should include:

- source kind (`PLAYER_IDEA`, `MAIA`, `STOCKFISH`, `LEELA_ALIEN_PROBE`, etc.);
- provider/display name;
- model or engine version when available;
- binary/model/weights identity when available;
- configuration summary;
- request ID or run ID;
- input position hash;
- observation/modeling timestamp when available;
- whether the move is observed externally, modeled locally, imported, or player-entered.

## CoachAssessment

An explanation generated from structured session data. It separates:

- observed facts;
- model predictions;
- engine calculations;
- LLM interpretation;
- practical coaching advice;
- uncertainty.

## Decision

The Player's selected move after reviewing analysis. A `Decision` is stored in the session and may be reviewed later. It is not an external platform action.

## PlatformCommand

A command that writes to an external chess platform, such as making a move, sending chat, accepting a challenge, resigning, or aborting. Platform commands are outside ordinary analysis and require their own future specification.

## HumanPath

The default training mode focused on practical play against human-like opposition. Opponent plies use Maia; Player-side continuations use Stockfish; the seed may be player-proposed or externally observed.

## RunRegistry

The persistence context for reproducibility. It stores run IDs, request IDs, provider metadata, configuration, timestamps, position hashes, raw transcripts where permitted, and final provenance references.

## Freeze / Ingest

The boundary step that converts raw input into immutable domain data. Examples: FEN parsing, SAN/UCI parsing, source enum normalization, timestamp canonicalization, and rejection of invalid shapes.
