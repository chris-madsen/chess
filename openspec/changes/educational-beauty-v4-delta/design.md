# Design

- Calibration stores state-level positive-near observations and has a checked-in freshness check.
- Lesson analysis replays the complete continuation before deriving retrospective dependencies and geometry.
- Pattern clarity uses calibrated family evidence only; `targetFamily` remains provenance for branch creation, never evidence.
- Sacrifice and motif labels require concrete board geometry or material evidence.
- Discovery, target branches, and plain CSTal baselines form one deduplicated lesson candidate set.
- The existing live renderer remains compact; the final renderer receives the selected lesson candidate.
